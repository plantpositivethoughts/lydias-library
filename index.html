How to use your journal

Adding books

  • Tap Add a book on your shelf.
  • Tap Scan the barcode and point the camera at the barcode on the back. The title, author, pages, genre and cover fill in for you.
  • Or add a photo of the front cover first: the journal reads the cover and suggests matching books to tap.
  • Tap the Add cover box to take a photo, choose one from your phone, or search for the real cover online.
  • Pick a shelf: Reading, Want to read, Finished or Did not finish. Adding a finished date puts it on Finished for you.
  • Changed your mind? Tap Cancel, Close, or anywhere outside the box.
  • Use the tabs and search bar above the shelf to find a book.

Collections (book series)

  • When adding or editing a book, type a Series name and its Book #. Books with the same series name are grouped together.
  • Tap Collections at the top of your shelf to see every series, how many you've read, and the series rating.
  • On a collection, reorder books with Up and Down, take one out with Remove, or tap Add books.
  • Rate the whole series yourself, or choose Average of the books to work it out from your book ratings. Write a review of the series too.
  • A book that's part of a series shows a Part of… link on its page.

Your book's page

  • Tap any book on your shelf to open its page.
  • Tap the stars for your rating. The glowing sixth star is for god tier books.
  • Tap the peppers for the spice rating, and pick how the book made you feel.
  • Your review saves as you type. Add favourite quotes and reading notes further down.
  • Mark as finished moves a book to Finished with today's date.
  • Edit details changes the title, dates or cover, or deletes the book.

Collages

  • On a book's page, tap Create collage. It starts with spaces ready to fill.
  • Tap an Add art space to add a photo or drawing from your phone, a book cover, free-to-use art, or something saved from Pinterest.
  • Tap an Add quote space to add a quote from any book you've saved quotes for.
  • Drag pieces to move them. Tap a piece for Smaller, Bigger, rotate, Front, Back, Style, Edit or Remove.
  • Layout gives a new arrangement, Sticker adds lace, tape, doilies and more, and Page changes the paper.
  • Draw lets you write or draw straight onto the collage with an Apple Pencil or your finger.
  • Tap Done when you're finished. Free art is credited under the collage for you.

Writing by hand

  • On a book's page, tap Write by hand for lined, dotted, grid or plain pages.
  • Choose Pen or Highlighter, a size and a colour. Pencil pressure makes lines thicker or thinner.
  • Once you use an Apple Pencil, finger drawing turns off so your hand can rest on the page. Turn it back on with Finger drawing.
  • Eraser removes whole lines. Undo and Redo fix mistakes, and New page adds another page.
  • Your pages show on the book's page. Tap one to carry on writing.

Diary & Calendar

  • Diary shows what you're reading now, what you read this month and everything you've read this year.
  • Calendar gives each month its own page with your books, favourite, a quote and your collages. The borders change with the seasons, or tap Frame to choose your own frame and lace.
  • Set a reading goal for the year at the top of the Calendar.
  • Tap any book in either one to jump to its page.

Getting around

  • Swipe left and right to move between Shelf, Diary, Calendar and Settings, or tap the buttons at the bottom.
  • On a book's page, the button at the top takes you back to where you came from.

Make it yours

  • Journal look in Settings switches between Cosy scrapbook, Romantasy and Fantasy.
  • Customise changes the background, colours, fonts, text size, star and pepper colours, and the bottom buttons.
  • On any book's page, Style gives that book its own look. Try Match the cover colours.

Keeping your journal safe

  • Your journal is saved only on this phone, in this browser. Nobody else can see it.
  • If you clear Safari's data or change phones, it will be lost unless you have a backup. Tap Export backup in Settings now and then, and keep the file somewhere safe.
  • To bring a backup back, tap Import backup and choose the file.
  • Pictures take the most space. Settings shows how much you've used.
  • Add the journal to your home screen to open it like an app (see Settings).
